Arial font subsets
==================

Files: Arial-Regular.ttf, Arial-Bold.ttf, Arial-Italic.ttf

These files are Latin-1/common-punctuation subsets of the Arial, Arial Bold and
Arial Italic font programs embedded in the Adobe-generated oracle PDFs under
test/fixtures/sample-forms/Adobe-generated-results/. They were produced with
fontTools; hinting was removed and the character set was limited to Latin-1 and
common punctuation (no Greek glyphs). They exist solely to reproduce the
metric-compatible Arial faces requested by XFA templates.
